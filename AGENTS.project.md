Silo Games is a Web 3 Gaming Studio. We exist to further the boundaries of Web3 gaming by building fun, economically sustainable Web3 games. 

Web 3 games have a poor track record of unsustainable economics. When playing is no longer profitable, players leave and games die. Silo Games is here to change that. 

Introducing Proof of Skill - earn by being good at playing games. PvP. May the best man win.
